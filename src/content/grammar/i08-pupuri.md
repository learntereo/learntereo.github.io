## Two kinds of "my"

Te reo Māori has two sets of words for "my", "your" and "his or her". The one you use depends on the thing.

- A forms: *tāku*, *tāu*, *tāna*
- O forms: *tōku*, *tō*, *tōna*

For more than one thing, drop the *t*: *āku* and *ōku*.

## Which one?

Use the O forms for family you were born into, your body, your clothes, your name, your home and things you ride in:

- *tōku whānau*, *tōku māmā*, *tōku ingoa*, *tō whare*

Use the A forms for things you have chosen or can control, such as your children, pets, food and belongings:

- *tāku kurī*, *tāku pukapuka*, *āku tamariki*

## Watch out

This is a guide, not a complete list. Parents take O (*tōku pāpā*) but your children take A (*āku tamariki*). When you are unsure, ask a teacher.
