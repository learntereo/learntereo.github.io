## Eating and drinking now

Use *Kei te*, the doing word, who is doing it, then *i* and the thing:

- *Kei te kai ahau i te āporo*: I am eating the apple
- *Kei te inu ia i te miraka*: he or she is drinking the milk
- *Kei te kai rātou i te kūmara*: they are eating the sweet potato

## What i does

The small word *i* comes before the thing that is eaten or drunk. It joins the action to its object.

## Saying something is good

*He pai te parāoa* means "The bread is good". This is the same pattern you used for colours: *He*, a describing word, *te*, the thing.

## Watch out

*Kai* is both "food" and "to eat". *Ia* means "he" or "she". Te reo Māori does not show gender in this word.
