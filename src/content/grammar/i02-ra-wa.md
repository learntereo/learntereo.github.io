## Days of the week

Every day name begins with *Rā*, which means day: *Rāhina* (Monday) to *Rātapu* (Sunday). Write them with a capital letter.

## On a day: past or future

The small word before the day tells you the time:

- *i te Rāhina*: on Monday, when it has already happened
- *ā te Rāapa*: on Wednesday, when it has not happened yet

For example:

- *I mahi ahau i te Rātū*: I worked on Tuesday
- *Ka haere tātou ā te Rātapu*: we will go on Sunday

## Parts of the day

*Ata* is morning, *ahiahi* is evening and *pō* is night. *Ata mārie* means "good morning".

## Watch out

Use *i* for past days and *ā* for future days. Do not swap them. *Āpōpō* (tomorrow) and *inanahi* (yesterday) go at the end of a sentence.
