## Describing things

To say what something is like, start with *He*, then the describing word, then *te* and the thing:

- *He kikorangi te moana*: the sea is blue
- *He kākāriki te rākau*: the tree is green

## Describing words come after the thing

When you name a thing and its colour together, the colour follows the noun:

- *rākau kākāriki*: a green tree
- *He rākau kākāriki tēnā*: that is a green tree

English puts the colour first. Te reo Māori puts it second.

## Watch out

*Te* means "the" for one thing. *Kei te ua* means "it is raining" (it is happening now). Do not use *he* in that one. Keep the macron on *mā* (white).
