## Where is it?

Use *Kei* to say where something is now, and *Kei hea* to ask:

- *Kei hea te pahi?*: where is the bus?
- *Kei te taone te toa*: the shop is in town
- *Kei te marae ngā tamariki*: the children are at the marae

## Going places

Use *ki* for the place you are going to:

- *Kei te haere ahau ki te hōhipera*: I am going to the hospital
- *Kei te haere mātou ki te tātahi*: we are going to the beach

## Watch out

*Kei te haere* means "is going" and *Kei te whare* means "is at the house". They look alike, so check the word after *Kei te*. If it is a doing word it is an action. If it is a place it is a location. *Mātou* means "we" when the listener is not included.
