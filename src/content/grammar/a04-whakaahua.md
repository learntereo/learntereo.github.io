## Describing words follow the thing

To describe a person, a place or a thing, put the describing word after the noun:

- *wahine ātaahua*: a beautiful woman
- *maunga teitei*: a high mountain
- *awa hōhonu*: a deep river

Add *He* at the start and a pointing word at the end to make a sentence:

- *He wahine ātaahua tērā*: that is a beautiful woman
- *He whare hou tēnei*: this is a new house

## Saying what something is like

You can also start with *He*, then the describing word, then *te* and the thing:

- *He ātaahua te whenua*: the land is beautiful

## Watch out

Say *ātaahua* with both *a* sounds long and clear. *Tawhito* is used for old things, and *pākeke* is for grown-up people. Do not use *tawhito* for a person.
