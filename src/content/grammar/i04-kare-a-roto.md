## How someone feels

Use *Kei te*, the feeling, then who feels it:

- *Kei te hiakai ahau*: I am hungry
- *Kei te mataku te kurī*: the dog is scared
- *Kei te māuiui te kuia*: the elderly woman is sick

## Very

*Tino* means "very" or "really". Put it straight before the feeling:

- *Kei te tino pai ahau*: I am very good
- *Kei te tino ngenge ahau*: I am very tired

## Watch out

*Kei te* is for how things are right now, such as feelings and conditions. For what something is like in general, use *He*: *He nui te whare*. Notice that *tino* comes before the feeling word, not after it.
