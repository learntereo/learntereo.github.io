## Asking what, who and where

Most questions start with a question word:

- *He aha tēnei?*: what is this?
- *Ko wai tō ingoa?*: what is your name? (literally, "who is your name")
- *Kei hea ngā tamariki?*: where are the children?
- *Nō hea koe?*: where are you from?

## Asking when

The word you choose shows the time:

- *Āhea* asks about the future: *Āhea koe haere ai?* (When will you go?)
- *Inahea* asks about the past: *Inahea koe i haere ai?* (When did you go?)

Notice the little word *ai* at the end of the verb in both. It goes after the doing word in these *when* questions.

## Watch out

Do not mix up *āhea* (future) and *inahea* (past). To ask "how", use *pēhea*. To ask "why", use *he aha ai*.
