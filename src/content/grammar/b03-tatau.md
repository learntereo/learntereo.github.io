## Counting things

To say how many things there are, use *E*, then the number, then *ngā*, then the thing:

- *E rima ngā manu*: there are five birds
- *E toru ngā kurī*: there are three dogs

To ask "How many?", use *E hia*: *E hia ngā ngeru?* means "How many cats?".

## Your age

*E hia ō tau?* asks "How old are you?". The answer uses *ōku tau*: *E ono ōku tau* means "I am six".

## Watch out

When you count out loud, say *tahi, rua, toru, whā*. To say "one" of something, use *kotahi*. *Kore* means zero or none. Keep the macron on *whā* (four).
