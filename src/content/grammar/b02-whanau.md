## This and that

To say "This is a ..." or "That is a ...", start with *He*, then the thing, then a pointing word:

- *He pēpi tēnei*: this is a baby (close to me)
- *He kuia tēnā*: that is an elderly woman (close to you)
- *He koroua tērā*: that is an elderly man (over there)

The three pointing words are *tēnei* (near me), *tēnā* (near you) and *tērā* (far from both of us).

## Naming people

Use *Ko* when you name someone: *Ko Hemi tōku pāpā* means "Hemi is my father".

## Watch out

*He* means "a" or "some". It does not change for one or many, so *He tamariki rātou* means "They are children". Put the pointing word last in the sentence.
