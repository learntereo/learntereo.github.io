## Words for the marae

A marae is more than a building. It includes the *marae ātea* (the open space in front), the *wharenui* (meeting house) and the *wharekai* (dining hall). The *tangata whenua* are the hosts and the *manuhiri* are the visitors.

## Saying what is happening

You already know *Kei te* for something happening now, and *Ka* for what will happen:

- *Kei te karanga te kuia*: the elderly woman is calling
- *Kei te whaikōrero te kaumātua*: the elder is giving a speech
- *Ka hongi ngā manuhiri*: the visitors will hongi

*Ka* is also used to say what happens next in a story or a ceremony.

## Watch out

*Tapu* means sacred or restricted, so *He tapu te wharenui* tells you to treat the meeting house with care. *Tikanga* and *kawa* differ between iwi and marae. Learn the ways of each place from the *kaumātua* and the hosts, and ask before you act.
