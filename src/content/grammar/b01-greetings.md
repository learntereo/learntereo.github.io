## Hello to one, two or many

*Tēnā koe* is a respectful hello. The last word changes with the number of people you greet:

- *Tēnā koe*: hello to one person
- *Tēnā kōrua*: hello to two people
- *Tēnā koutou*: hello to three or more people

*Kia ora* works for everyone, at any time.

## Saying how you are

Ask with *Kei te pēhea koe?* (How are you?) and answer with *Kei te* plus a feeling, then who is feeling it:

- *Kei te pai ahau*: I am good
- *Kei te harikoa ahau*: I am happy

## Saying your name

Use *Ko* before a name: *Ko Mere tōku ingoa* means "My name is Mere".

## Watch out

*Haere rā* is said to the person who is leaving. *E noho rā* is said to the person who is staying. Keep the macrons: they change how a word sounds.
