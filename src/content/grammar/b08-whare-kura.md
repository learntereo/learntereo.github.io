## Asking where something is

Use *Kei hea* and then the thing:

- *Kei hea te wharepaku?*: where is the toilet?
- *Kei hea te kaiako?*: where is the teacher?

## Big and small

*Nui* means big and *iti* means small. Use the same pattern as colours:

- *He nui te whare*: the house is big
- *He iti te tēpu*: the table is small

## Going somewhere

*Kei te haere ahau ki te kura* means "I am going to school". The small word *ki* means "to" or "towards".

## Watch out

*Kura* is "school". *Kaiako* is a teacher and *ākonga* is a student. *Hea* only appears in questions about place, so it always comes straight after *Kei*.
