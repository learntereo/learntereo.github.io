## Weather words

Weather words are used in a few patterns:

- *E ua ana*: it is raining
- *E hukarere ana*: it is snowing
- *Kei te wera te rā*: it is a hot day

## Weather as a description

For what the weather is like, use *Kei te* with a describing word:

- *Kei te mahana te wai*: the water is warm
- *Kei te marino te moana*: the sea is calm

## Big things

Use *He nui te ...* to say something is big or strong: *He nui te hau* means "The wind is strong".

## Watch out

*E ... ana* tells you the weather is happening, as in *E ua ana*. *Kei te* with a describing word tells you what it is like, as in *Kei te wera te rā*. Say each vowel clearly: *mahana*, *maroke*, *marino*.
