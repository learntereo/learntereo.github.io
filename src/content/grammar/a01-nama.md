## Building the bigger numbers

For 11 to 19, say *tekau mā* and then the digit:

- *tekau mā tahi*: eleven
- *tekau mā rima*: fifteen

For the tens, say the digit first and then *tekau*:

- *rua tekau*: twenty
- *toru tekau*: thirty
- *rua tekau mā tahi*: twenty-one

For bigger numbers, *rau* is a hundred and *mano* is a thousand: *kotahi rau* (100) and *kotahi mano* (1000).

## Counting things and people

Count things with *E*, the number and *ngā*: *E rua tekau ngā ākonga* means "There are twenty students". For people, the number gets *toko* in front: *tokorua*, *tokotoru*, *tokowhā*. To ask, say *Tokohia ngā tāngata?*

## Watch out

In *tekau mā tahi*, *mā* means "and". It is a different word from *mā*, the colour white. With *kotahi*, leave out *E*: *Kotahi rau ngā manu*.
