## The, a and the plural

- *te* means "the" for one thing: *te kurī* (the dog)
- *ngā* means "the" for more than one: *ngā kurī* (the dogs)
- *he* means "a" or "some": *he kurī* (a dog)

The noun itself does not change. Only the small word before it shows one or many.

## Something happening now

Use *Kei te* and a doing word, then who is doing it:

- *Kei te kai te kurī*: the dog is eating
- *Kei te kai ngā kurī*: the dogs are eating

## Watch out

Do not add an "s" to make a plural. *Ngā manu* is "the birds" and *manu* stays the same. Say *kiwi* and *pūkeko* with the vowels short and clear.
