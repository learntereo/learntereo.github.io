## Wearing and carrying

*Mau* means to wear, carry or hold. Use it with *Kei te* and *i*:

- *Kei te mau ia i te pōtae*: he or she is wearing the hat
- *Kei te mau ahau i ngā hū*: I am wearing the shoes

## Describing clothes

The pattern for describing is the same as for colours: *He*, the describing word, *te*, the thing.

- *He whero te koti*: the coat is red
- *He kikorangi te tarau*: the trousers are blue

## Finding things

*Kei hea te pēke?* means "Where is the bag?".

## Watch out

*Hū* is both one shoe and many shoes, so check the word before it: *te hū* is one shoe and *ngā hū* is several. Clothes words like *tarau* and *tōkena* do not change for plurals either.
