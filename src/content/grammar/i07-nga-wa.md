## Tense markers

Te reo Māori shows time with a small word at the start of the sentence. The doing word itself does not change.

- *I*: it already happened. *I kai ahau i te āporo* (I ate the apple)
- *Ka*: it will happen. *Ka kai tātou i te parāoa* (We will eat bread)
- *Kei te* or *E ... ana*: it is happening now. *Kei te kai ahau* (I am eating)

## Time words

Words like *inanahi* (yesterday) and *āpōpō* (tomorrow) usually go at the end:

- *I haere ahau ki te toa inanahi*: I went to the shop yesterday
- *Ka haere ahau ki te kura āpōpō*: I will go to school tomorrow

## Watch out

A past sentence about being somewhere also begins with *i*: *I te kura ahau inanahi* means "I was at school yesterday". You will also meet *Kua* later, for something already finished.
